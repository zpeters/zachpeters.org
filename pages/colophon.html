Styling

  • Tufte CSS, Edward Tufte uses a distinctive style in his handouts: simple, with well-set typography, extensive sidenotes, and tight integration of graphics and charts. tufte-css brings that style to HTML documents.

Typefaces

  • ET Book, with old-style figures, for body text and headings
  • Palatino, Georgia and serif as fallbacks
  • Consolas, Menlo and monospace for code
