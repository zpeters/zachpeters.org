Choose Boring Technology

🗄️ https://mcfunley.com/choose-boring-technology

Whenever I'm starting a new project and i have just the thing that will solve the problem, I always think back to this article. It's a common trait among software engineers to keep chasing the rush from learning new tools and systems. This reminds me to embrace the boredom and think about optimizing globally rather than in one particular spot. Meaning that the understandability of a system can be just as important as raw speed.
